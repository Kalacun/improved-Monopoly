# Chance and Community Chest

The decks use the familiar 16 classic US Chance outcomes and the traditional 16-card Community Chest set. The code keeps the previously saved card indices stable. Each effect is applied by the game engine; the card descriptions are concise, project-written wording rather than scans or transcriptions of printed cards.

The Community Chest outcomes match the traditional set used from 2008 through 2021. Hasbro later rewrote that deck for newer editions, so cards vary by edition. This project preserves the classic set that matched its previous gameplay.

Chance covers advances to GO, Illinois Avenue, St. Charles Place, Reading Railroad and Boardwalk; two nearest-railroad cards; a nearest-utility payment; a dividend; a jail-release card; back-three and go-to-jail moves; property repairs; a speeding fine; a payment to each opponent; and a matured building loan.

Community Chest covers GO, bank error, doctor and stock payments, jail release and jail, holiday fund, income-tax refund, birthday collections, life insurance, hospital and school fees, a consultancy payment, street repairs, a beauty prize and an inheritance.

All monetary effects follow the game's optional inflation index. A release card stays out of its deck while held, can be traded in a deal, and returns when used. Other cards return to the bottom of their deck after drawing.

New cards open a reader for the drawing player. Pass-and-play shows all draws. Chained movement can draw two cards; **Next card** lets you read both. Effects apply once without a reading timer. The Turn/TV panel retains the latest face during that turn and hides it when the turn ends. **Read recent cards** retains history. Old saves without draw-turn metadata fall back to the card log.

The effects and card counts are based on the classic US set description at [Monopoly Land](https://www.monopolyland.com/list-monopoly-chance-community-chest-cards/). Hasbro's [current classic product listing](https://instructions.hasbro.com/en-us/instruction/monopoly-board-game-classic-game-with-storage-tray-and-larger-tokens-family-games-8) confirms that the game includes 16 Chance and 16 Community Chest cards.

Both decks are translated into Slovenian. Classic US property and railroad names stay in English so they match the board. Private old saves can still contain earlier historical card wording; releases ship no saved games.
