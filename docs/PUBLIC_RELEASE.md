# Public-release identity and rights review

Estate Exchange is an independent property-trading game, distributed free of charge. The user has chosen to restore the familiar square board and US place-name sequence. This document records the remaining similarity concerns; it does not guarantee that no one will bring a claim, constitute permission from a rights holder, or replace advice from a lawyer in the jurisdictions where it is distributed.

## What changed

- The product name is Estate Exchange. Public copy does not market it as an official version of another game.
- At the user's request, the square 40-space board and classic US property-name sequence have been restored. The board uses project-created central artwork and graphics; it does not include publisher logos or scans of printed cards.
- Space labels use the familiar US street, railroad and utility names in either interface language. The UI can still be displayed in Slovenian. Chance and Community Chest use the classic US effects with concise, project-written card descriptions; no printed card artwork or scans are included.
- Drone, Lighthouse, Fox, Comet, Crystal and Robot replace the recognizable six-piece selection. Their geometry is created by this project. Old saved figure identifiers render the new counterparts for compatibility; they do not load old replica assets. Previous public figure exports were removed.
- Third-party sound, font, software, model and runtime notices are retained. User uploads and all private hosting data are excluded from source/release uploads.
- MIT applies to project-authored work; it does not relicense third-party content or grant another publisher's trademark rights.

## Why free distribution is not enough

The [US Copyright Office's guidance on games](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html) distinguishes game ideas/methods from protectable literary or pictorial expression. The [USPTO explains trademark infringement](https://www.uspto.gov/page/about-trademark-infringement), including confusion and famous-mark dilution. Hasbro's own [published notice](https://www.hasbro.com/common/assets/image/Printables/dad288661c4311ddbd0b0800200c9a66/3BA69B8250569047F513A43FE38BE736/3BB6FBBA50569047F53476FB46A7ECAE.pdf) asserts rights in names, logos, board design, corner spaces and playing pieces. The restored square arrangement and classic US place-name sequence increase the visual and naming similarity to that game; this project has not received permission or legal clearance for that similarity.

These are US sources, not a definitive analysis of Slovenian/EU or worldwide law. Keeping a project free, adding a disclaimer or describing it as a fan project does not itself establish a license or eliminate copyright/trademark concerns. The custom branding and artwork do not resolve the increased similarity caused by the restored board and names; professional review is still appropriate before a broad public launch. The Estate Exchange name itself has not undergone comprehensive trademark clearance.

## Before publishing

Review the final visuals, repository history and all assets; retain their licenses. Check the provenance of any newly contributed models, screenshots, music or art. Do not reintroduce another publisher's branded card scans, board graphics, token replicas or edition names as a downloadable “theme.” New user-uploaded figures require the user's own sharing rights.

Past local saves may contain old labels in historical logs/cards. They are private user data and are not copied into releases. Do not publish screenshots or fixture files from personal saved games. Stable field names such as `chance`, `chest`, `jailed` and `classic` are internal compatibility identifiers, not advertised product/deck names.
